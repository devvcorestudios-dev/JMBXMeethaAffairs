Since 1935
A Legacy of Sweetness

Taste, Trust, and Tradition

What began as a humble dream in the late 1920s, lives on today as a legacy of taste, trust and tradition. Our journey began with Shri Bapulal Ji Jain, a first-generation entrepreneur who walked the streets of Indore, serving freshly made poori sabji and moong dal halwa in pure desi ghee. As his passion found recognition, our first outlet opened in Sarafa in 1935.

In 1953, Jain Mithai Bhandar was established by Shri Mangilal Ji Jain, a turning point that shaped generations. Rooted in quality and consistency, it soon became a trusted name in Indore's rich food culture.

As the city evolved, so did we—expanding from sweets to namkeen and modern indulgences, while staying true to our promise of excellence. Milestones like the Moti Tabela store in 1985 and the landmark Pipliyahana store in 2015 became part of this growing legacy.

Today, as tastes evolve and gifting becomes an expression of emotion, a new chapter unfolds — Meetha Affairs.
